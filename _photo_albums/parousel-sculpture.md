So many ideas, so many thoughts; running through my mind as I walk through life.

This was the first time I actually *completed* the photos for a project I envisioned. I am happy that it was completed, and as much as I had hoped I could automate art...I couldn't. This will be refined, modified, edited, and deployed. It might be better, but it might suck.

Regardless of how it currently looks, I took a walk through a city in which I was a stranger. I got 3 mangos and a nectarine on my way to take these pictures. The local folks I talked to didn't know what I was talking about when I described these sculptures. A little secret that hasn't blurred into the background for me.

I haven't found any actual information regarding these items, and I'm okay with that. There is a...wornness to it all. These items existed in a place where they were used for their purpose, and often, the wear is a testament to it. And once that was done, they were all removed, bolted to a jagged pole, and erected to be ignored by all.
