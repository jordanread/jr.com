Marc Ferrez spent four decades turning his camera on Guanabara Bay —
its harbor traffic, its beaches, and the granite peaks that ring it —
from vantage points around Rio de Janeiro including Pão de Açúcar and
Corcovado. He was Brazil's leading 19th-century photographer and served
as official photographer to the Imperial Navy.

Ferrez died in 1923; his work is in the public domain everywhere. The
images here are hosted on Wikimedia Commons and linked directly rather
than mirrored locally — see each image's Commons file page for full
attribution and source detail.
