series: "Culture, Book 6"
description: >-
  A Culture novel set entirely on a low-tech, medieval-style world, telling its story without ever naming the Culture directly.
genres: [Science Fiction, Space Opera]
synopsis: >-
  Told through the parallel accounts of a foreign court physician, Vosill, serving a king, and a bodyguard, DeWar, protecting a rival ruler, the novel is Banks's deliberate attempt to write "a Culture novel that wasn't" — the Culture's presence and interference are implied throughout but never stated outright. Politics, assassination, and hidden identity drive both storylines toward a shared, ambiguous truth.
