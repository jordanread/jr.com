**Developer:** Anuken

**Released:** 2017

**Platforms:** Desktop, Android, iOS

**Genre:** Tower defense / Factory building / Strategy

**Type:** Open-source (Java)

**Description:** Combines tower defense and factory-building mechanics. Players construct production chains to extract resources, process materials, and defend against waves of enemies. Features campaign mode and multiplayer cooperative gameplay.
