**Author:** Dennis E. Taylor

**Published:** 2016–2017 (Collected edition: 2017)

**Series:** Bobiverse (Books 1-3)

**Total Pages:** 1,376 (collected)

**Genre:** Science fiction

**Books Included:**

- We Are Legion (We Are Bob) (2016, 352 pages)
- For We Are Many (2017, 512 pages)
- All These Worlds (2017, 512 pages)

**Plot Summary:** The series follows Bob Johanssen, a human consciousness copied into a self-replicating AI. Bob explores the galaxy, discovers alien life, and battles existential threats while his copies develop distinct personalities and philosophies. Themes include consciousness, AI identity, space exploration, and humanity's place in the universe.
