Horza is dispatched on a mission to recover a renegade Culture Mind that has taken refuge on a forbidden world, drawing him and a crew of mercenaries into a series of increasingly desperate schemes across a galaxy at war. It's Banks's first published science fiction novel and the introduction to the Culture universe.
