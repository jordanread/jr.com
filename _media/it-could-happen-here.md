synopsis: >-
  The podcast began in 2019 when Robert Evans consulted civil war scholars, military officers, former federal agents, and State Department strategists after becoming concerned about escalating political violence. All agreed that a second American civil war was plausible. The original nine-part series walked listeners through how such a conflict could unfold—examining the triggers (political violence spiraling beyond containment), the cascading fracturing of political parties into competing factions, and the mechanisms of state collapse. Evans drew on his firsthand reporting from Iraq and Ukraine to ground the scenarios in real-world precedent. The show later relaunched as a daily program, evolving from hypothetical analysis into a chronicle of contemporary collapse and practical exploration of how to build resilient communities amid systemic breakdown.
